🧪 What Sandbox Mode does
Safe testing for your email sequences
You turn it on for a campaign
Open any campaign → the ⚙️ gear icon → Sandbox Mode.
Real leads receive nothing
Every send in that campaign is intercepted before delivery — your actual leads are never contacted.
You get the test email instead
Redirected to your inbox, subject prefixed with [TEST SANDBOX], with all personalization (like "Hello John") rendered exactly as a lead would see it.
Everything else runs normally
Delays, step order, and workflow progression behave exactly like a real send — only the destination changes.
